# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA supplements the Terms of Service when Lexicon Flow processes personal data on behalf of a customer.

- **Processor:** Lexicon Flow LLC  
- **Controller:** The customer (you)  
- **Purpose:** Provide and support the Lexicon Flow SaaS platform.  
- **Sub-processors:** Supabase, Clerk, Stripe, Google Analytics.  
- **Security Measures:** Encryption, access controls, limited retention.  
- **Data Transfers:** Processed in the United States.  
- **Deletion:** Upon termination or written request, data deleted within 60 days.
