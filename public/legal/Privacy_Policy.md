# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Info:** name, email, password.  
- **Billing Info:** handled by **Stripe**.  
- **Usage Data:** via **Google Analytics** and logs.  
- **Project Data:** files and configuration stored in **Supabase**.

## 2. Use of Information
Used to provide, operate, and improve Lexicon Flow; process payments; communicate updates; ensure security; and comply with laws.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
Data stored securely in **Supabase (U.S.)** with encryption and access controls.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use **Clerk**, **Stripe**, **Supabase**, and **Google Analytics**. Each has independent privacy policies.

## 5. Cookies & Tracking
Cookies help maintain sessions and measure engagement. Manage cookies via your browser.

## 6. Data Retention
Data is retained while accounts are active or legally required.

## 7. Your Rights
You may request data deletion or copies via **support@lexicon-flow.com**.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Not intended for individuals under 18.

## 9. Updates
We may revise this Policy periodically and notify users of significant changes.
